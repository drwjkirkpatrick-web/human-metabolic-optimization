# Clinically Ranked SNPs by Actionability

A prioritized list of pharmacogenomic variants ranked by clinical impact, evidence strength, and actionability. Use this for targeted learning and clinical decision-making.

---

## 🚨 TIER 1: CRITICAL (Immediate Action Required)

*CPIC Level 1A/1B evidence. These variants have mandatory testing recommendations or contraindications that can be life-saving.*

### 1. DPYD (Dihydropyrimidine Dehydrogenase)
| Variant | rs Number | Drugs | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|-----------|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **DPYD *2A** | rs3918290 (IVS14+1G>A) | 5-FU, capecitabine, tegafur | **Fatal toxicity risk** | **Contraindicate** fluoropyrimidines |
| **DPYD *13** | rs55886062 (p.Ile560Ser) | 5-FU, capecitabine | Severe toxicity | Dose reduction 50% or avoid |
| **HapB3** | rs75017182 (c.1129-5923C>G) | 5-FU, capecitabine | Splicing defect | Dose reduction 50% |
| **c.2846A>T** | rs67376798 (p.Asp949Val) | 5-FU, capecitabine | Reduced activity | Consider alternative therapy |

**Ranking justification**: DPYD *2A has the highest clinical stakes—administering 5-FU to a carrier can cause fatal neutropenia, mucositis, and diarrhea. CPIC guideline recommends contraindication (strongest possible action).

### 2. TPMT (Thiopurine S-Methyltransferase)
| Variant | rs Numbers | Drugs | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|------------|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **TPMT *2** | rs1800462 (p.Gln239Ter) | 6-MP, 6-TG, azathioprine | **Fatal myelosuppression** | Avoid or extreme dose reduction (10%) |
| **TPMT *3A** | rs1800460+rs1142345 (p.Ala154Thr+Tyr240Cys) | 6-MP, 6-TG, azathioprine | No enzyme activity | Avoid or extreme dose reduction |
| **TPMT *3C** | rs1142345 (p.Tyr240Cys) | 6-MP, 6-TG, azathioprine | Reduced activity | Dose reduction 30-70% |

**Ranking justification**: Second-highest stakes—standard thiopurine dosing in poor metabolizers causes fatal bone marrow suppression. CPIC mandatory pre-prescription testing recommended.

---

### 3. HLA-B*57:01
| Variant | Test Method | Drug | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|-------------|------|-----------------|--------|
| **HLA-B*57:01** | PCR/SSO | Abacavir | **Fatal hypersensitivity** | **Contraindicate** abacavir if positive |

**Ranking justification**: FDA black box warning. 100% specificity for immunologically confirmed hypersensitivity reaction. Mandatory testing before abacavir initiation.

---

### 4. HLA-B*15:02 / HLA-A*31:01
| Variant | Population | Drug | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|------------|------|-----------------|--------|
| **HLA-B*15:02** | Han Chinese, Thai | Carbamazepine | **Stevens-Johnson syndrome** | Contraindicate in at-risk populations |
| **HLA-A*31:01** | European | Carbamazepine, phenytoin | **SJS/TEN, DRESS** | Consider alternative anticonvulsants |

**Ranking justification**: Life-threatening cutaneous adverse reactions. Population-specific screening recommended by FDA.

## 💊 TIER 2: HIGH (Major Dosing Changes)

*CPIC Level 1A/1B. These variants require significant dose adjustments or alternative drug selection.*

### 5. CYP2C19 (Clopidogrel)
| Variant | rs Number | Drug | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|-----------|------|-----------------|--------|
| **CYP2C19 *2** | rs4244285 (p.Pro227Leu) | Clopidogrel | **No activation to active drug** | Use prasugrel or ticagrelor instead |
| **CYP2C19 *3** | rs4986893 (p.Trp212Ter) | Clopidogrel | No enzyme function | Use alternative antiplatelet |
| **CYP2C19 *17** | rs12248560 | Clopidogrel, PPIs | Ultra-rapid metabolism | Standard clopidogrel; consider PPI alternatives |

**Ranking justification**: Affects millions of patients post-PCI. Poor metabolizers have increased cardiovascular events on clopidogrel. CPIC recommends alternative P2Y12 inhibitors.

---

### 6. CYP2D6 (Opioids & Psychiatry)
| Variant | rs Numbers | Drugs | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|------------|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **CYP2D6 *3** | rs35742686 | Codeine, tramadol, tamoxifen | No enzyme | **Avoid codeine** (ineffective) |
| **CYP2D6 *4** | rs3892097 | Codeine, tramadol, tamoxifen | No enzyme | **Avoid codeine** |
| **CYP2D6 *5** | Gene deletion | Codeine, tramadol, tamoxifen | No enzyme | **Avoid codeine** |
| **CYP2D6 *10** | rs1065852 (p.Pro34Ser) | Codeine, tramadol | Reduced function | Avoid codeine; reduce tramadol |

| **CYP2D6 *2xN** | Gene duplication | Codeine, tramadol | Ultra-rapid | **Avoid codeine** |

**Ranking justification**: Codeine deaths in children (CYP2D6 UM) led to FDA black box warning. Affects pain management and psychiatric medication efficacy.

### 7. CYP2C9 + VKORC1 (Warfarin)
| Variant | rs Number | Drug | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|-----------|------|-----------------|--------|
| **CYP2C9 *2** | rs1799853 (p.Arg144Cys) | Warfarin | Reduced clearance | Reduce dose 20-40% |
| **CYP2C9 *3** | rs1057910 (p.Ile359Leu) | Warfarin | Markedly reduced clearance | Reduce dose 30-50% |
| **VKORC1** | rs9923231 (-1639G>A) | Warfarin | Increased sensitivity | Reduce dose 20-40% |

**Ranking justification**: Warfarin is high-risk medication. Combined CYP2C9/VKORC1 testing can reduce bleeding events and time to therapeutic INR.

### 8. G6PD (Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase)
| Variant | Type | Drugs | Clinical Impact | Action |
|---------|------|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **G6PD Mediterranean** | Class I | Primaquine, dapsone, rasburicase | **Hemolytic anemia** | Contraindicate oxidant drugs |

| **G6PD Canton** | Class II | Primaquine, dapsone, rasburicase | Hemolytic anemia | Contraindicate oxidant drugs |

**Ranking justification**: Essential for malaria treatment (primaquine) and oncology (rasburicase). Pre-prescription testing standard of care.

### 9. UGT1A1 (Irinotecan)
| Variant | rs Number | Drug | Clinical Impact | Action |
