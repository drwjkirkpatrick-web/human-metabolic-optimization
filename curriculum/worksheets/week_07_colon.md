## Part 2: Clinical Consequences by Genotype

**LPL deficiency / reduced function:**
| Outcome | Clinical Context |
|---------|-----------------|
| Hypertriglyceridemia | Pancreatitis risk (>1000 mg/dL) |
| Chylomicronemia | Eruptive xanthomas, lipemia retinalis |
| Response to fibrates | Generally good |
| Response to omega-3 | Variable — high doses needed |
| Alcohol sensitivity | Absolute contraindication |

**APOE ε4:**
| Outcome | Risk |
|---------|------|
| Late-onset Alzheimer's | 3-4× risk per ε4 allele |
| Statin response | Variable; may require higher intensity |
| LDL receptor binding | Reduced clearance → higher LDL |
| Dietary fat sensitivity | Saturated fat → stronger LDL rise |
| Response to dementia prevention | Strongest evidence for lifestyle intervention |

**APOE ε2:**
| Outcome | Risk |
|---------|------|
| Type III hyperlipoproteinemia | Homozygous ε2/ε2 + metabolic stress |
| Hypobetalipoproteinemia | Lower LDL naturally |
| Statin myopathy | Potentially higher risk |

## Part 3: Precision Interventions

**For LPL deficiency / borderline:**
- [ ] **Very low fat diet** (<20g/day) if severe
- [ ] **Omega-3 EPA/DHA** → 2-4 g/day (prescription Lovaza or Vascepa)
- [ ] **Fibrates** (gemfibrozil, fenofibrate) → LPL transcriptional activators
- [ ] **Niacin** → reduces triglyceride synthesis (use cautiously)
- [ ] **Medium-chain triglycerides (MCT)** → bypass chylomicron pathway
- [ ] **Complete alcohol abstinence**

**For APOE ε4 carriers:**
- [ ] **Mediterranean diet** — strongest evidence for AD prevention
- [ ] **Low saturated fat** (<7% calories) — ε4 is most fat-sensitive
- [ ] **Omega-3 DHA** → 1000 mg/day for brain structure support
- [ ] **Exercise** → particularly important for hippocampal volume
- [ ] **Sleep hygiene** — glymphatic clearance is critical
- [ ] **Curcumin / resveratrol** — blood-brain barrier permeant antioxidants

**For PPAR-gamma Pro/Pro (standard risk):**
- [ ] **Thiazolidinediones** → excellent drug response (rosiglitazone, pioglitazone)
- [ ] **Exercise** → PPAR-gamma is exercise-responsive
- [ ] **Chromium picolinate** → insulin sensitivity support
- [ ] **Alpha-lipoic acid** → mitochondrial glucose disposal

---

## Clinical Pearl
> APOE ε4 carriers respond to dietary saturated fat almost linearly — a single high-fat meal can raise LDL by 30-50 mg/dL in ε4/ε4 individuals, while ε3/ε3 carriers show minimal change. This is why ε4 carriers benefit most strictly from Mediterranean dietary patterns. The "ApoE4 Diet" is not a fad — it's a genetically matched eating pattern.
