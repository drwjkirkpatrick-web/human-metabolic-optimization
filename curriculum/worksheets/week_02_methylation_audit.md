## Part 3: Recommended Cofactor Stack

- [ ] **L-Methylfolate (5-MTHF)** → Bypass MTHFR bottleneck
- [ ] **Methylcobalamin (B12)** → Direct MTR substrate
- [ ] **P5P (B6)** → CBS and BH4 synthesis cofactor
- [ ] **Riboflavin (B2)** → MTHFR flavin cofactor (FMN/FAD)
- [ ] **Magnesium** → ATP-dependent remethylation reactions
- [ ] **Betaine (TMG)** → Alternative remethylation pathway via BHMT

**Dosing framework:**
- 5-MTHF: 400–800 mcg (titrate from low; anxiety history → start at 200 mcg)
- Methylcobalamin: 1000 mcg sublingual or IM
- P5P: 25–50 mg
- B2: 10–25 mg
- Mg: 200–400 mg glycinate or malate

---

## Clinical Pearl
> The "methyl trap" occurs when 5-MTHF is synthesized but cannot be remethylated to methionine due to B12 deficiency or MTR/MTRR variants. Folic acid can worsen this by flooding the cycle without enabling the remethylation step. Always test MMA before high-dose folate.
