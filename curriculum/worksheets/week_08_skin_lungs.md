## Part 2: Clinical Consequences

**COMT Met/Met ("Worrier" — low dopamine clearance):**
| Outcome | Clinical Context |
|---------|-----------------|
| Stress response | Higher, more sustained cortisol/dopamine peaks |
| Pain sensitivity | **Higher pain perception** → may need higher opioid dosing |
| Estrogen dominance | Slower estrogen metabolism → PMS, fibroids, cancer risk |
| Cognitive style | Better sustained attention; worse cognitive flexibility |
| Psychiatric | Higher anxiety risk; better response to dopaminergic meds |
| Response to yoga/meditation | Stronger neuroplastic response |

**COMT Val/Val ("Warrior" — high dopamine clearance):**
| Outcome | Clinical Context |
|---------|-----------------|
| Stress resilience | More resilient under acute stress |
| Pain sensitivity | **Lower pain threshold** → may under-report pain |
| Cognitive style | Better cognitive flexibility; worse focus under stress |
| Substance abuse risk | Higher (rapid dopamine clearance = seeking behaviors) |
| Response to stimulants | Greater benefit from methylphenidate, amphetamines |

**SOD2 Ala16Val (rs4880):**
| Genotype | MnSOD Activity | Clinical Risk |
|----------|---------------|---------------|
| Val/Val | Higher | Better mitochondrial defense; lower CV risk |
| Ala/Val | Intermediate | Standard risk |
| Ala/Ala | Lower | **Higher oxidative damage risk** — prioritize Mn, SOD boosting |

**MPO -463G>A (rs2333227):**
| Genotype | MPO Expression | Risk |
|----------|---------------|------|
| GG | High | Higher lung injury, atherosclerosis, COPD risk |
| GA | Intermediate | Intermediate risk |
| AA | Low | **Lower MPO-dependent damage** — cardioprotective |

## Part 3: Precision Interventions

**For COMT Met/Met (low activity):**
- [ ] **Magnesium** → COMT cofactor; supports catalytic efficiency
- [ ] **SAMe** → methyl donor for dopamine degradation
- [ ] **B6 (P5P)** → cofactor for dopamine synthesis and degradation
- [ ] **Avoid excessive dopamine precursors** (L-tyrosine, Mucuna) unless indicated
- [ ] **Mindfulness-based stress reduction** → works neurobiologically with slower clearance
- [ ] **Magnesium + taurine** → GABA support to counter sympathetic peaks

**For COMT Val/Val (high activity):**
- [ ] **L-tyrosine** → dopamine substrate support
- [ ] **Mucuna pruriens** → natural L-DOPA source
- [ ] **B6 + iron** → dopamine synthesis cofactors
- [ ] **Consider stimulant medication** for ADHD if applicable
- [ ] **Exercise** → endogenous dopamine boost
- [ ] **Avoid excessive methyl donors** (SAMe, TMG) — may deplete dopamine further

**For SOD2 Ala/Ala (low MnSOD):**
- [ ] **Manganese** → 2-5 mg elemental Mn (not to exceed 11 mg/day)
- [ ] **SOD-rich foods** → barley grass, wheatgrass, cantaloupe
- [ ] **Mitochondrial antioxidants** → CoQ10, PQQ, alpha-lipoic acid
- [ ] **Avoid excessive iron** → Fenton reaction risk with low SOD2

**For MPO GG (high myeloperoxidase):**
- [ ] **Cruciferous vegetables** → sulforaphane inhibits MPO
- [ ] **Curcumin** → direct MPO inhibition
- [ ] **Vitamin C** → scavenges hypochlorous acid (MPO product)
- [ ] **No smoking** → massive MPO activation via neutrophil recruitment
- [ ] **Omega-3** → reduces neutrophil activation

## Clinical Pearl
> The COMT Val158Met polymorphism is a classic case of "no free lunch." Met/Met carriers have better prefrontal cortex function, attention, and emotional regulation but pay with higher stress reactivity and pain sensitivity. Val/Val carriers are more stress-resilient but less focused and more impulsive. Understanding this lets you match interventions to the individual's neurobiology rather than prescribing the same stress-management or pain protocol to everyone.
