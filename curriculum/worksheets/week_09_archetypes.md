## Part 2: Archetype Classification

**Explorer (GSTM1/GSTT1 Null, often APOE ε4):**
| Trait | Clinical Implication |
|-------|---------------------|
| Chemical sensitivity | Avoids strong scents, cleaning products, pesticides |
| Environmental reactivity | Mold illness, MCS “ canaries ” |
| Detox priming essential | NAC + selenium before ANY mobilization |
| Dietary preference | Organic, low-pesticide; benefits from cruciferous |
| Exercise tolerance | May be lower due to oxidative burden |
| Caffeine response | Often slow (CYP1A2) |

**Thrifty (APOE ε4, PPAR Pro/Pro, FTO TT):**
| Trait | Clinical Implication |
|-------|---------------------|
| Efficient fat storage | Gains weight easily on standard diet |
| Dietary fat sensitivity | Saturated fat markedly raises LDL |
| AD risk | Highest; lifestyle intervention is preventive medicine |
| Exercise response | Needs MORE exercise for equivalent metabolic benefit |
| Fasting tolerance | Poor; hypoglycemia risk |
| Insulin resistance | Earlier onset; prevent with lifestyle |

**Nomad / Lean (APOE ε2, FTO AA, MC4R protective):**
| Trait | Clinical Implication |
|-------|---------------------|
| Natural leanness | May be underweight; needs calorie density |
| Fat malabsorption risk | ε2/ε2 → monitor for malabsorption |
| Statin myopathy risk | Higher; consider alternatives |
| Fasting tolerance | Better; may benefit from intermittent fasting |
| Muscle building | May need more effort |

## Part 3: Archetype-Specific Protocol Design

**Explorer Protocol:**
- [ ] **Priming mandatory** → NAC 600 mg, selenium 200 mcg for 2 weeks before mobilization
- [ ] **Environmental remediation** → HEPA air filter, low-VOC home, organic produce
- [ ] **Cruciferous vegetables daily** → sulforaphane upregulates remaining GSTs
- [ ] **Alpha-lipoic acid** → recycles glutathione independently of GST
- [ ] **Low-and-slow detox** → never large single doses of mobilizing agents
- [ ] **Sauna / exercise caution** → Herxheimer risk; short sessions, adequate hydration

**Thrifty Protocol:**
- [ ] **Mediterranean diet** → APOE ε4-specific evidence
- [ ] **Low saturated fat** (<7% calories); emphasize MUFA/PUFA
- [ ] **DHA 1000 mg/day** → brain structural support for AD prevention
- [ ] **Exercise 150+ min/week** → minimum for ε4 metabolic homeostasis
- [ ] **Sleep optimization** → glymphatic clearance critical for β-amyloid
- [ ] **Statin discussion** → if needed, use high-intensity; monitor cognition
- [ ] **Curcumin / resveratrol** → BBB-permeant anti-inflammatory
- [ ] **Fasting NOT recommended** → worsens hypoglycemia, stress response

**Nomad / Lean Protocol:**
- [ ] **Calorie-dense nutrition** → nuts, avocado, olive oil if underweight
- [ ] **Statin alternatives** → red yeast rice, berberine, ezetimibe if needed
- [ ] **IF/time-restricted eating** → may be beneficial and well-tolerated
- [ ] **Protein adequacy** → 1.0-1.2 g/kg for muscle preservation
- [ ] **Monitor lipid status** → ε2/ε2 can flip to type III dyslipidemia under stress

## Clinical Pearl
> The "Explorer" archetype is not just a personality type — it's a biochemical reality. GSTM1/GSTT1 Null individuals have measurably higher levels of environmental chemical biomarkers (BPA, phthalates, organophosphates) in urine. Their clinical presentation of "chemical sensitivity," "mold illness," and "autonomic dysfunction" often reflects a real INABILITY to conjugate xenobiotics. Treating this as a constitutional weakness rather than a psychiatric condition is the foundation of functional medicine.
