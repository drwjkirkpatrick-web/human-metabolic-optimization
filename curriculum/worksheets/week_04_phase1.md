## Part 2: Critical Drug-Gene Interactions

**For CYP2D6 PM / IM:**
| Drug | Action Required |
|------|-----------------|
| Codeine / Tramadol | **Avoid** — cannot convert to morphine (ineffective analgesia) |
| Tamoxifen | **Avoid** — cannot form endoxifen (reduced efficacy) |
| Metoprolol / Carvedilol | **Consider dose reduction 50%** |
| SSRIs (fluoxetine, paroxetine) | **Use lower dose / alternative** |
| Amitriptyline / Nortriptyline | **Avoid TCA / use therapeutic drug monitoring** |

**For CYP2C19 PM:**
| Drug | Action Required |
|------|-----------------|
| Clopidogrel | **Avoid** — form prasugrel or ticagrelor |
| Voriconazole | **Use standard monitoring** |
| PPIs (omeprazole, esomeprazole) | **Consider lower dose** |
| Diazepam | **Use lower dose** |

**For CYP2D6 UM:**
| Drug | Action Required |
|------|-----------------|
| Codeine / Tramadol | **Avoid** — rapid morphine formation → toxicity |
| Tricyclic antidepressants | **Avoid** — rapid metabolism → subtherapeutic |

## Part 3: Phase I Cofactors and Modulators

**Cofactors required for CYP450 function:**
- [ ] **Iron (heme)** — prosthetic group for all CYPs
- [ ] **NADPH** — electron donor (from NAD(P)H-cytochrome P450 reductase)
- [ ] **Molecular oxygen** — terminal oxidant
- [ ] **Flavin adenine dinucleotide (FAD)** — cytochrome P450 reductase cofactor
- [ ] **Flavin mononucleotide (FMN)** — same enzyme

**Natural CYP inducers (use cautiously):**
- St. John's Wort → CYP3A4, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 potent induction
- Ginkgo biloba → moderate CYP induction
- Garlic → CYP3A4 mild induction

**Natural CYP inhibitors (exacerbate PM phenotype):**
- Grapefruit → CYP3A4 potent inhibition
- Berberine → CYP2D6, CYP3A4, CYP2C9 inhibition
- Goldenseal (berberine) → broad CYP inhibition
- Quercetin → moderate mixed inhibition

## Clinical Pearl
> Activity scores: PM = 0, IM = 0.5–1, EM = 1–2, UM > 2. A CYP2D6 *1/*1xN (gene duplication) has activity score ≥ 3 and is classified as ultra-rapid. For gene duplication carriers on codeine, **morphine levels can reach 4× normal**, creating serious respiratory depression risk.
