# 📖 Master Glossary: Applied Emunctorology & Pharmacogenomics

A centralized reference for the terminology used throughout the 12-week course.

## 🧬 Genetics & SNPs
- **SNP (Single Nucleotide Polymorphism):** A variation in a single DNA base pair. These act as "volume knobs" for enzyme production.
- **Null Allele:** A genetic variation that results in the complete absence of a functional protein/enzyme (e.g., GSTM1 Null).
- **Epigenotype:** The expression of genes influenced by environmental factors, identifying a "metabolic archetype" (e.g., Warrior, Explorer).
- **Pharmacogenomics:** The study of how genes affect a person's response to drugs.

## 🧪 Metabolic Pathways
- **One-Carbon Cycle:** The biochemical pathway responsible for methylation, utilizing Folate and B12 to regulate DNA and neurotransmitters.
- **Phase I Detox (Oxidation):** The process (mostly via CYP450) that makes toxins more water-soluble, often creating reactive intermediates.
- **Phase II Detox (Conjugation):** The process (via GST, NAT2, UGT) that neutralizes Phase I intermediates and prepares them for excretion.
- **Glucuronidation:** A Phase II pathway (UGT enzymes) that attaches glucuronic acid to toxins to make them excretable via bile or urine.

## 🚽 Emunctorology
- **Emunctories:** The primary organs of elimination (Skin, Lungs, Kidneys, Liver, Colon).
- **Herxheimer Reaction:** A "healing crisis" occurring when toxins are mobilized faster than the emunctories can eliminate them.
- **Metabolic Rigidity:** The inability of a system to efficiently switch fuel sources or clear waste, often linked to PPAR/LPL SNPs.

## 💊 Clinical Terms
- **Cofactor:** A non-protein chemical compound (vitamin/mineral) required for an enzyme's biological activity.
- **P-gp (P-glycoprotein):** An efflux pump (MDR1) that pushes drugs and toxins out of cells and back into the intestinal lumen.
